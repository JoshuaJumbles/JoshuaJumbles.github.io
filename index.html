01 · About

I work mostly solo, across iOS/Swift installations and Unity/C# games — turn-based systems, hex and maze generators, gesture-first mechanics, and the occasional client build for a small business kiosk. I like tight scopes and fast iteration: most of these repos started life as a paper prototype or a jam entry.

Recently I've been pulled toward board-game architecture — generalized turn-based engines that can seat any ruleset on top — and toward UI/UX component systems for my own workflow.
